Delete your account & data

You can permanently delete your Padel Rivals account and its associated data at any time. Choose whichever option is easier for you.

Option 1 — By email (works without the app)

Send us a deletion request from the email address on your account. We verify it belongs to you, then permanently remove the account.

Email a deletion request

Or email us directly at Padelrivals@gmail.com.

Option 2 — From inside the app (instant)

  1. 1 Open Padel Rivals and go to the You tab.
  2. 2 Tap Privacy & Account.
  3. 3 Under Danger Zone, tap Delete Account and confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately and you're signed out.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, contact details)
  • Your rating, ranking history and placement progress
  • Your matches, tournament entries and community memberships
  • Your saved addresses and notification preferences

What we keep, and for how long

For legal, accounting and fraud-prevention reasons we retain a limited record of completed orders (cash-on-delivery / transfer purchases) for up to 24 months. These records are de-identified from your deleted profile and are not used to contact you.

How long it takes

In-app deletions are processed promptly. Email requests are actioned within 48 hours and completed no later than 30 days from the request.

Questions? Contact Padelrivals@gmail.com.
