    Questions

    Do I need to download the app?

    No. You can register and pay here. The app is optional — it shows your bracket, results and level.

    How do I know the InstaPay account is really yours?

    Only pay to the InstaPay address shown on this page at padel-rivals.com — never to one sent in a private message. Unsure? Message us on WhatsApp first.

    What if I can't make it?

    If you withdraw before the tournament's start date, your entry fee is refunded the same way you paid. Withdrawing on or after the start date forfeits the fee. See our terms.

    What do the levels mean?

    Levels go from D (newer players) up to A (strongest), each split into Low, Mid and Upper. Each tournament only lists the levels it's open to.

    I don't have a partner yet.

    No problem — choose "Find me a partner" in the form. We'll match you with another player at your level, introduce you both on WhatsApp before the event, and you only pay your own share. If we can't find you a partner in time, your fee is refunded in full.
